Cyber Safety Sticker Studio

Design a reward sticker for the Cyber Safety Test. Choose a badge theme, describe what you want on it, then generate artwork with your own OpenAI account and download it as a PNG.

1 Connect your OpenAI key

Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to api.openai.com. It is never saved to the project or shared with anyone else. Use a key with a spending limit, and remove it when you are finished.

2 Design the sticker

Short words work best. Long captions are often misspelt by the image model.

3 Output options

gpt-image-1 supports transparent backgrounds but needs a verified OpenAI organisation. Switch to dall-e-3 if you get a verification error.

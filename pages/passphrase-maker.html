What is a passphrase?

A passphrase is a password made from several unrelated words instead of one short, complicated word.

A typical password

@ 0 1
P@ssw0rd1

9 characters

Short, full of awkward symbols, hard to remember, and the substitutions are exactly what guessing software tries first.

A passphrase

🌊 🦍 🍉 🇯🇵
Ocean-Gorilla-Watermelon-Japan

30 characters

Much longer, easy to picture in your head, and far harder to guess because the words have nothing to do with each other.

Length beats complexity. Every extra character multiplies the work an attacker has to do.

Pictures stick. You remember a gorilla in the ocean far better than a string of symbols.

Unrelated is the point. Words that belong together are easier to guess.
